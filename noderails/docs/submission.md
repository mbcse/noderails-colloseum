# NodeRails

Production-grade crypto payment infrastructure for one-time and recurring payments on Flow and Filecoin.

## Live now
- Website: https://www.noderails.com
- Merchant app: https://merchant.noderails.com
- Docs: https://www.noderails.com/docs

## Vision
NodeRails is built for startups, apps, AI agents, and internet businesses that want to accept crypto without building payment operations from scratch.

Our product focus is speed, reliability, and business-ready recurring payments.

## Recurring subscriptions, built for real businesses
NodeRails is designed as a production-grade recurring subscription stack on modern chains, with a strong focus on Flow and Filecoin.

You can:
- Create subscriptions quickly from a clean merchant dashboard
- Run recurring billing flows with clear payment lifecycle visibility
- Track payment states and customer actions in one place

## Core payment flow
1. Create a checkout session, payment link, invoice, or subscription
2. Share with customer
3. Customer pays through a fast hosted checkout
4. Payment is tracked from authorization to capture to settlement
5. Merchant gets status, records, and operational visibility

## Pay in one token, settle in another
NodeRails is built to support flexible token flows across supported chains.

Merchants can offer customer-friendly payment options while configuring their preferred settlement setup. In practice: pay in one token, receive in another token flow, including support across Flow and Filecoin ecosystems.

## Accept any token strategy
We support broad token acceptance patterns so teams can go live faster.

If you launched your own token, you can:
- Set it as a payment token
- Use it for campaign pricing
- Offer token-based discounts and incentives
- Build community-native payment experiences around your asset

## Flow + Filecoin first-class support
Flow and Filecoin are a key part of this product direction.

Why this matters for merchants:
- Fast and transparent on-chain payments
- Reliable rails for recurring and one-time transactions
- Verifiable payment history for operations and reporting

## Dispute handling built in
NodeRails includes a dispute handling layer so merchants can manage payment disagreements with a structured workflow instead of ad hoc support threads.

This helps teams move from manual resolution to consistent, trackable outcomes.

## Fraud and compliance engine
NodeRails includes a fraud and compliance engine so merchants do not need to build their own risk operations stack.

The platform handles fraud/compliance workflows in the background, reducing operational burden and helping teams focus on growth instead of risk tooling.

## Why source is not fully public
We are actively building this startup, and our server-side infrastructure is a core part of our competitive advantage.

Because of that, we are not sharing the complete backend source publicly at this stage. We share product capabilities, live experience, and integration surfaces, while keeping core server implementation private.
